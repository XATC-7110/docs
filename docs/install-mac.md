# Install on a Mac

!!! info "Coming soon"
    The Mac app is **not available yet**. The pilot site's Download page says "The Mac app is coming soon" until it is. This page describes what installing will look like, so you know what to expect.

The Mac app will be for **Apple silicon Macs (M1 or later)**.

## What installing will look like

1. Sign in to the pilot site, [app.7110atc.com](https://app.7110atc.com), open **Download** and choose the Mac download.
2. Open the downloaded `.dmg` file.
3. Drag **xatc-agent** to your **Applications** folder.
4. Open **xatc-agent** from Applications. It asks to use the microphone; allow it.

After that you [sign in and choose your audio](getting-started.md#sign-in) as on every platform. Updates will work as on the other platforms: a banner offers **Update now**, never during a flight (see [Updates](settings.md#updates)).

Until the Mac app is out, the app runs on [Windows](getting-started.md) and [Linux](install-linux.md).
